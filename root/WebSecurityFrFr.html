Browsing the web securely is important!

A web app can access: Your language, If you have an internet connection, Your approximate location, and What kinds of features your browser is capable of.


Sources For Learning More About Online Security:

  • George Mason University - "How do I safely browse the internet?" This contains lots of browser-related advice.
  • Official Google Advice - "Tips and tools to help you stay safer online." This resource goes over several common types of attacks and unsafe behaviors.
  • America's Cyber Defense Agency - "Tips to Stay Safe while Surfing the Web." This resource has a whole training on Cybersecurity, though I am only sharing part 1, which is for recommended Web Browser Settings.
